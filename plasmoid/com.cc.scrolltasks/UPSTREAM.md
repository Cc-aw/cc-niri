# Upstream basis

This applet is a minimal fork of KDE Plasma Desktop's Task Manager applet at
tag `v6.7.5` (`43f55fff3480c6eb5f60133f045bfc74b9c67d84`). Its original SPDX
headers and GPL-2.0-or-later licensing are preserved. Phase 8.5 changes are
limited to UUID-based logical Column order synchronization and its IPC guard.
